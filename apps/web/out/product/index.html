THE EXECUTION PLATFORM

Configure the process.
AmazFlow runs it.

AmazFlow is the governed execution layer between your people and software: one control plane for workflows, policies, approvals, exceptions, AI decisions, agents, and audit history.

01

Observe

Understand the authorized page, queue, file, message, or event that begins the work.

02

Decide

Apply deterministic policies and bounded AI decisions to choose from allowed next actions.

03

Act

Execute through the browser, a spreadsheet, an API, email, files, or a human task.

04

Verify

Read the resulting state and confirm that the requested outcome actually happened.

05

Escalate

Route ambiguity, policy thresholds, and failures to the right person with context.

06

Audit

Record the who, what, when, why, method, authorization, and result of every material action.

CONFIGURATION OVER CUSTOM CODE

Build the workflow once. Execute it anywhere.

Workflows describe the outcome, conditions, approvals, and evidence required. Execution providers determine whether the step runs through an authorized browser session, spreadsheet, API, file, inbox, or human queue. That separation keeps the process reusable as systems change.
